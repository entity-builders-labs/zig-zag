# What to do in Caminito

### **What to Do in Caminito: The Most Colorful Corner of Buenos Aires**

Caminito, located in the neighborhood of La Boca, is one of the most iconic destinations in Buenos Aires. Its colorful facades, its history tied to tango, and its artistic atmosphere make it a must-visit spot for both locals and international tourists.

In this article, we’ll tell you what to do in Caminito, how to make the most of your visit, and some practical tips to enjoy it.

#### **Walk Through the Colorful Streets**

Caminito’s main attraction is its brightly painted houses, a tradition that began with Genoese immigrants who used leftover paint from ships to decorate their homes. Every corner is perfect for photos and for soaking in the spirit of Buenos Aires. Join our [La Boca Walking Tour](https://bafreetour.com/walking-tour-la-boca/) to learn the history of these streets.

#### **Enjoy Live Tango Performances**

On nearly every corner, you’ll find dancers performing tango in the open air. You can simply watch, take photos, or even try a few steps yourself. Many restaurants and bars also offer live music shows.

#### **[what to do in caminito](https://bafreetour.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/caminito-la-boca-1.webp)Explore Street Art and Galleries**

Caminito is also an open-air museum. Murals, sculptures, and works by local artists decorate the streets. There are also galleries and workshops where you can buy unique paintings and handicrafts.

#### **Visit the La Boca Fine Arts Museum – Quinquela Martín**

Just a few meters from Caminito is this museum dedicated to the artist Benito Quinquela Martín, a key figure in the cultural identity of the neighborhood. His work reflects the lives of port workers and is an excellent complement to your visit.

#### **Shop for Souvenirs and Handicrafts**

From Boca Juniors jerseys to tango-themed keepsakes, Caminito is a great place to pick up a typical Buenos Aires souvenir. Local markets offer handmade products and collectible items.

#### **Try the Local Cuisine**

In the parrillas (grills) and traditional eateries of the area, you can try Argentine classics like empanadas, choripán, or asado. It’s also a good opportunity to taste local wines or enjoy mate with pastries.

#### **Visit La Bombonera**

If you’re a football fan, you can’t miss the Boca Juniors stadium, known as La Bombonera. It’s very close to Caminito and offers guided tours and a museum to learn more about the club’s history. [Learn more in this article.](https://bafreetour.com/visit-la-bombonera/)

#### **Practical Tips for Visiting Caminito**

* **Best time to visit:** During the day, especially in the morning when there are fewer tourists.
* **Safety:** It’s best not to stray too far from the tourist area and to avoid staying after sunset.
* **Getting there:** You can reach Caminito by taxi, bus, or organized tours.
* **Ideal duration:** 2 to 3 hours is enough to explore the area at a relaxed pace.

Caminito is not just a stroll — it’s a unique cultural experience that blends history, art, tango, football, and cuisine. Without a doubt, it’s a place that should be on your Buenos Aires itinerary.
