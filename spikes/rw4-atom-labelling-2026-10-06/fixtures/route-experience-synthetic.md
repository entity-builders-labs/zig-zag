## The Painted Lane walk

Start at Harbour Square, next to the old fire station.
Walk down Mill Street to reach the Painted Lane.
The Painted Lane is the reason to come: stroll its whole 150-metre curve slowly, looking at every painted tin house and the murals along it.
Halfway along, you will pass the Sailors' Church on your right.
At the end of the lane, visit the Riverside Art Museum.
